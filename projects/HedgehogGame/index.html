Hedgehog
Apple Catcher

A fun and charming arcade game where you control a hedgehog to catch falling apples. Built with Python & Pygame, featuring smooth physics, playful animations, and juicy particle effects.

Core Stack Python & Pygame
Features Physics, Particles, Levels
Platform Desktop & Web

Play Now

Experience the game right here in your browser. Use the left and right arrow keys to move the hedgehog and catch the falling apples!

⌨️ Arrow Keys to Move • Catch apples to score • Press R to restart

Game Mechanics

Every detail—from the hedgehog's waddling walk to the satisfying burst of particles when an apple is caught—is crafted to create a polished, delightful experience.

Smooth Physics

Acceleration, friction, and bouncy wall collisions create natural, responsive movement.

Living Animations

The hedgehog waddles when running and breathes when idle. Apples rotate and pulse as they fall.

Juicy Particles

Golden burst on catch, red splash on miss — particles with gravity and fade for satisfying feedback.

How to Play

Simple controls, deep engagement. The game progressively challenges you with faster apples and a new environment as you level up.

Catch & Score

Use arrow keys to move the hedgehog left and right. Each caught apple earns 10 points. Miss one and lose a life!

Level Progression

Reach 200 points to unlock Level 2 — a new night environment with faster falling apples and restored lives.

5 Lives System

You start with 5 lives. When they run out, it's game over — but press R to jump right back in!
